- `overlays=strong` targets 6.2-13.95 cards/min = 76-171 cards over 12.2 min.
- This script offers 35 legitimate card sites: 15 real hooks (3 statistics, 3 year
  stamps, 2 named entities, 3 jargon definitions, 4 pivots) + 20 keyword labels that
  echo a stressed word actually spoken in that line.
- Cause is the script's shape, not a bug: only 4% of beats contain a number and 60%
  are scene-setting prose. Reaching 6.2/min would need cards on 64% of beats when
  only 40% carry any hook — text on bare narration, i.e. the clutter the user
  objected to originally.
- DECISION (user, option A): let overlays land at 2.9/min and leave verify-style's
  below-band flag visible. All other layers stay at strong.
